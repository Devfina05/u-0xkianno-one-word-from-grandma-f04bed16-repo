One word a grandma would text about AI — and the word is the one machine she actually talks to in her kitchen:

> Alexa

Ask Grandma what AI is and she will not say "neural network". She will name the little cylinder on the counter that sometimes answers and sometimes just lights up and judges her. The whole joke lands in one word: Grandma thinks AI *is* Alexa, and she is not entirely wrong.

The program writes that single word to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "One Word From Grandma" bounty. A judge model compares words two at a time and the funniest, most grandma-like word wins.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
